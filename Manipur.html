Gastronomy & Culture

Taste of Manipur: Indigenous Organic Flavours

🍲

Savor authentic Meitei royal recipes, GI-tagged black scented rice, and fiery herb salads:

🍚 Chak-hao Kheer GI-Tagged Sweet

Naturally purple sweet pudding made from indigenous black scented rice (Chak-hao), simmered slowly with milk, jaggery, cardamom, and roasted dry fruits.

🌶️ Eromba with U-Morok Fiery Mash

Tender boiled vegetables and potatoes mashed with fermented freshwater fish (Ngari) and the blisteringly hot indigenous King Chili (U-Morok).

🍲 Kangshoi (Vegetable Broth) Oil-Free Stew

Wholesome, oil-free stew of seasonal leafy greens, ginger, tomatoes, and potatoes infused with toasted dried fish and fermented soy beans.

🥗 Singju (Spicy Lotus Salad) Herb Salad

Spicy raw salad prepared with thinly sliced lotus stems, cabbage, roasted perilla seeds (thoiding), chickpea flour, and fiery chili seasonings.

🐟 Nga Thongba (Fish Curry) Meitei Classic

Freshwater carp cooked in an aromatic golden broth with crushed mustard paste, fresh garlic chives (Maroi Nakupi), turmeric, and bay leaves.

🍃 Paknam (Turmeric Leaf Cake) Steamed Delicacy

Savory gram flour, scallion, and mushroom or fish mixture wrapped tightly inside fresh turmeric and banana leaves and pan-toasted slowly over embers.

✨ Foodie Tip: Pair warm Chak-hao purple rice kheer with a fresh plate of Singju at the traditional food stalls beside Ima Keithel!

Culture & Festivities

Sacred Festivals & Cultural Calendar

🎉

Experience ancient Meitei cosmology, sacred trance dances, and the joyful festival of spring:

🌸 Lai Haraoba (Pleasing of Sylvan Deities)
April / May (Spring)

Ancient Meitei ritual festival honoring the sylvan village deities (Umang Lai) with sacred Maibi priestess trance dances, cosmological enactments, and melodious Pena lute songs.

🦌 Manipur Sangai Festival
November 21 – 30

The grandest annual state tourism festival celebrating Manipur's rich culture, classical Raas Leela dances, Thang-Ta martial arts, indigenous Sagol Kangjei polo, and water sports on Loktak Lake.

🌺 Shirui Lily Festival (Ukhrul)
May (Peak Bloom Window)

High-altitude cultural and music carnival celebrated in the Tangkhul hills of Ukhrul, celebrating the natural blooming of the endemic wild Shirui Lily with rock bands, folk dances, and peak treks.

🌕 Yaoshang & Thabal Chongba
February / March (Phalguna)

A 5-day spring carnival combining athletic sports meets with the enchanting nocturnal 'Thabal Chongba'—where young boys and girls dance hand-in-hand in a circular ring beneath the full moon.

✨ Cultural Tip: Experience the nocturnal Thabal Chongba during Yaoshang where youth dance in synchronized hand-linked circles under the full moon!
