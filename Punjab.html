Gastronomy & Culture

Taste of Punjab: Hearty Dhaba & Tandoor Flavours

🍲

Savor authentic wood-fired tandoor breads, slow-cooked mustard greens, and creamy butter curries:

🌽 Makki di Roti & Sarson da Saag Winter Classic

Slow-simmered winter mustard greens, bathua, and spinach cooked in earthen pots, served with yellow cornmeal flatbread, homemade white butter, and jaggery.

🫓 Amritsari Kulcha & Chole Tandoori Icon

Flaky, multi-layered tandoor-baked leavened flatbread stuffed with spiced potatoes and pomegranate seeds, crushed by hand with melting desi ghee and tangy chole.

🍲 Dal Makhani (Slow-Cooked Lentils) Overnight Simmer

Whole black urad lentils and red kidney beans slow-simmered overnight over charcoal embers, finished with butter, fresh dairy cream, and kasuri methi.

🍗 Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani) Global Star

Charcoal-grilled tandoori chicken pieces cooked gently in a velvety, silky smooth gravy of fresh tomatoes, rich cream, churned butter, and aromatic spices.

🐟 Amritsari Machhi (Crispy Fish) Spiced Fry

Freshwater river sole or singhara fish marinated with crushed carom seeds (ajwain), ginger-garlic paste, and coated in gram flour batter, deep-fried crispy.

🥛 Amritsari Sweet Cream Lassi Frothy Elixir

Thick, velvety yogurt churned by hand with sugar and crushed cardamom, served in tall clay glasses (kulhad) topped with a thick slab of fresh clotted cream (malai).

✨ Foodie Tip: Enjoy warm, buttery Langar Mahaprasad at the Golden Temple's Sri Guru Ram Das Langar Hall, which feeds over 100,000 visitors daily!

Culture & Festivities

Sacred Festivals & Cultural Calendar

🎉

Experience the founding of the Khalsa, equestrian mock battles, and joyous winter harvest bonfires:

🌾 Baisakhi (Vaisakhi Festival)
April 13 / 14

The supreme cultural and spiritual festival commemorating both the winter wheat harvest and the historic creation of the Khalsa Panth in 1699 by Guru Gobind Singh Ji, celebrated with vibrant Bhangra and Gidda dances.

⚔️ Hola Mohalla (Anandpur Sahib)
March (Chet Month)

A thrilling 3-day martial extravaganza founded by Guru Gobind Singh Ji where blue-robed Nihang warrior Sikhs showcase horse riding acrobatics, tent-pegging, Gatka sword battles, and music.

🔥 Lohri (Winter Bonfire Festival)
January 13 (Maghi Eve)

Joyous winter festival marking the passing of the winter solstice, celebrated across villages with communal evening bonfires, singing folk songs of Dulla Bhatti, and sharing jaggery rewri, peanuts, and popcorn.

✨ Parkash Utsav Guru Nanak Dev Ji
November (Kartik Purnima)

The sacred birth anniversary of Guru Nanak Dev Ji, founder of Sikhism. Golden Temple is illuminated with thousands of lamps and fireworks, accompanied by 48-hour continuous Akhand Path readings.

✨ Cultural Tip: Witness the spectacular display of martial arts (Gatka) and equestrian tent-pegging by Nihang Sikhs during Hola Mohalla in Anandpur Sahib!
