Gastronomy & Culture

Taste of Assam: Culinary Heritage & Orthodox Tea

🍲

Explore traditional Assamese flavours, indigenous river dishes, and world-famous orthodox teas:

🍵 Omita Khar (Alkaline Starter)

The defining dish of Assamese meals: raw papaya cooked with natural alkaline liquid filtered through sun-dried wild banana peel ashes (Kola Khar) and mustard oil.

🍵 Masor Tenga (Tangy Fish Curry)

Refreshing light fish broth cooked with fresh Brahmaputra carp, tomatoes, lemon juice, or elephant apple (Ou Tenga) for warm tropical afternoons.

🍵 Duck with Ash Gourd & Pitha

Ahom royal specialty (Hanh aru Kumura) slow-cooked with ash gourd and black pepper, accompanied by coconut and jaggery-stuffed Til & Ghila Pitha.

🍵 Mishing Pork & Assam Orthodox Tea

Pork slow-cooked with fermented bamboo shoot (Khorisa) and King Chilli in Majuli stilt homes, paired with malty golden-tipped Jorhat orthodox tea.

✨ Dining Tip: Start your traditional Assamese thali with Omita Khar and finish with malty Jorhat orthodox black tea!

Culture & Festivities

Sacred Festivals & Cultural Calendar

🎉

Time your journey with Assam’s colorful seasonal festivals, river rituals, and theatrical plays:

🎭 Rongali (Bohag) Bihu
Mid-April • Across Assam

The Assamese New Year celebrating spring with dhol beats, buffalo-horn pepa, and graceful Bihu dancing in traditional Muga silk attire.

🎭 Ambubachi Mela (Kamakhya)
Mid-June • Nilachal Hill

South Asia's premier Tantric gathering celebrating Mother Earth's fertility as millions of devotees gather at Kamakhya Devi Temple.

🎭 Majuli Raas Leela & Magh Bihu
Nov & Jan • Majuli & Upper Assam

Island-wide Krishna theatrical plays with Samaguri clay masks in November, followed by Magh Bihu harvest bonfires (Meji) in January.

✨ Planning Advice: Book Majuli homestays months ahead if visiting during the vibrant Raas Leela Mahotsav in November!
